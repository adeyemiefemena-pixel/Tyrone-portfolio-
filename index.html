How I work

I read your last 100 customer messages first.It tells me more than a kickoff meeting does. I see what people really ask, and what your team keeps typing out by hand.

I launch a small version fast.Usually within two weeks. Real customers use it, and we fix what they trip over before adding anything new.

A person can always step in.When the bot is stuck, your team gets the whole conversation and takes over. I don't build bots that pretend they can handle everything.
